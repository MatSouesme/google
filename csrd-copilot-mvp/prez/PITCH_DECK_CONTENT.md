## **NOTES POUR LA PRÉSENTATION ORALE (10 MIN)**

### **Timing recommandé:**
- Slides 1-2 (Problem): **1 min 30s** - Hook fort, chiffres chocs
- Slide 3-4 (Context): **1 min** - Établir CSRD comme problème inévitable
- Slide 5-6 (Solution): **2 min** - Démo rapide du workflow + Dual-Core AI
- Slide 7-8 (Market/Business): **2 min** - TAM/SAM/SOM + business model
- Slide 9-12 (GTM/Growth): **2 min** - Différenciation + stratégie
- Slide 13-15 (Traction/Ask): **1 min 30s** - Roadmap + appel à l'action

### **Tips pour le pitch:**

1. **Commencer fort:**
   "Imagine spending EUR 320,000 and 500 hours on a report nobody reads—except auditors looking for mistakes. That's CSRD for 50,000 EU companies."

2. **Démo visuelle:**
   Si possible, montrez 10 secondes de l'interface (upload Excel → AI draft généré)

3. **Ancrer dans le réel:**
   "Volkswagen's 2023 sustainability report? 200 pages. Our AI analyzed it in seconds to benchmark your draft."

4. **Montrer la page Schneider (SLIDE 10):**
   "We don't pitch generic. Look at this: a custom page for Schneider Electric. Their numbers. Their savings. Auto-generated from public data. That's not a pitch—that's a business case."

5. **Finir sur l'urgence:**
   "CSRD reports are due NOW for large companies. We're not early—we're exactly on time."
